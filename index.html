TalkTrack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Who we are

TalkTrack is a voice-powered personal expense tracker for iPhone. It is developed and operated as an independent app. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at claasmueller2@icloud.com.

What data TalkTrack collects and why

1. Expense data (financial information)
Your expenses, budgets, categories, and recurring entries are stored on your device using Apple's secure local database. This data never passes through our servers. You may optionally enable:

  • iCloud Sync — stores your data in your own private Apple iCloud account, subject to Apple's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Worker Sync — if you choose to configure this advanced feature, data is sent to a Cloudflare Worker URL that you provide and control. We have no access to it.

2. Voice and microphone data
When you record an expense by voice, your iPhone's on-device speech recognition converts your speech into text. The raw audio is never stored or transmitted anywhere. The resulting text transcript is sent to an AI language model (via the OpenRouter API) solely to identify the expense amount, category, and description. The transcript is not stored by us beyond that single API call.

3. Account information (email address and user ID)
If you create an account, your email address and a unique user ID are stored with our authentication provider (Supabase). This is used only to secure your account and is never shared with advertisers or analytics providers.

4. Purchase and subscription information
Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat. TalkTrack never directly handles payment information. Please refer to RevenueCat's privacy policy for details on how subscription data is processed.

What TalkTrack does NOT do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not share your expense data with third parties (except the AI categorization call described above, which is stateless and not stored).
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect analytics or crash reports beyond what Apple provides to all App Store developers via App Store Connect.
