Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

TalkTrack is a voice-powered personal expense tracker for iPhone. It is developed and operated as an independent app.

What data we collect and why

1. Expense data: Stored on your device using Apple's secure local database. Optional iCloud Sync or Cloudflare Worker Sync (user-controlled) can be enabled.

2. Voice data: On-device recognition converts speech to text. Raw audio is never stored. Text transcripts are sent to the OpenRouter API for categorization but are not stored by us beyond that call.

3. Account info: Email and User IDs are stored via Supabase to secure your account. Not shared with advertisers.

4. Purchase info: Managed by RevenueCat. We do not handle payment details directly.
